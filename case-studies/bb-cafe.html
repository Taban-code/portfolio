Making the café easier to discover.

B & B Café needed a digital presence that could introduce the business and help visitors find their way around its offering. The original project brief described the website as a way to streamline online menu browsing and customer ordering, while keeping the experience modern and responsive.

The goal was practical: make the café feel approachable online, present key information clearly and make the next step easy to find on desktop and mobile.

The brief

For a local café, a website should quickly answer the questions a potential customer has before visiting or ordering. The project called for a clean presentation of the café and a browsing experience that did not feel cumbersome on smaller screens.

Clear discoveryGive visitors a simple way to explore what the café offers.
Responsive experienceKeep the experience usable across phone, tablet and desktop layouts.
Ordering pathwayMake it easier for customers to move from browsing toward an order.
Brand atmosphereUse a modern visual presentation suited to an artisanal café.

My approach

I approached the project as a customer journey: introduce the café, make the menu and offering easy to reach, then make the next action clear. The design direction prioritised readable content, simple navigation and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes.

The site was built in Wix Studio, which provided the visual site-building environment for shaping and publishing the responsive experience.

What I delivered

The original portfolio describes the finished work as a functional, responsive and modern café website. The work centred on the public-facing website and its menu-browsing and ordering journey.

This case study intentionally does not claim sales growth, order volume or conversion improvements: no analytics or client outcome figures were available to verify those results.

What this project reinforced

Small-business websites work best when the essentials are easy to find. For a café, the experience should be quick to understand and comfortable to use on a phone. This project strengthened my focus on clear information hierarchy, responsive layouts and designing around the visitor's next action.
