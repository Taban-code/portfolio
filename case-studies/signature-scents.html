Turning a fragrance catalogue into a shopping experience.

Signature Scents is a perfume and cologne e-commerce project. Unlike a simple brochure website, an online store has to help visitors move from browsing a range of products to understanding an item and continuing through a purchase journey.

The original portfolio described the project as a modern e-commerce website with fragrance filtering, product galleries and a shopping cart. Those capabilities shaped the case-study focus: product discovery, presentation and a clear shopping flow.

The brief

Fragrance is a highly visual product category. Online, shoppers rely on the way products are organised and presented to narrow their choices. The store needed to make browsing feel manageable and provide the familiar building blocks of an online shop.

Product discoveryHelp shoppers browse and narrow down the available fragrance range.
Product presentationGive products a visual space that supports comparison and exploration.
Shopping journeyConnect product selection to a cart-based purchase flow.
Store managementUse an established commerce platform to manage the catalogue and store content.

My approach

I structured the experience around the main stages of shopping: browse the catalogue, explore product details, then add chosen items to the cart. The visual direction aimed for a sleek, contemporary feel appropriate to perfume and cologne retail.

WordPress provided the content management foundation, WooCommerce supplied the e-commerce layer, and Elementor was used to build and style the website pages.

What I delivered

The original project description identifies the main elements as fragrance filtering, product galleries and a shopping cart experience. The implementation stack was WordPress, WooCommerce and Elementor.

What this project reinforced

E-commerce design is about more than displaying products. Catalogue structure, useful product information and a predictable path through the cart all contribute to a store that feels easier to use. This project developed my experience combining a visual page builder with a dedicated commerce platform.
